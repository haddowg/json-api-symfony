# Writes require the JSON:API Content-Type and actions negotiate their body against what they accept

Every `POST`, and any other write with a body, now has to say what it sends (core ADR 0172): CRUD writes, Atomic Operations and `None` or `Document` actions must send `application/vnd.api+json`, while a `Raw` action matches its body against its `accepts` list, or against any type but the three cross-origin form types when it has none. An action may list several media types and read each in its own mode, because one upload endpoint often takes a CSV, an image or a document, and the matched type is handed to the handler. A bodyless `POST` sent as JSON:API passes every action, since clients send that type on every `POST` and it is never sent cross-origin without a preflight; refusing it would force clients to special-case actions whose body is optional.
